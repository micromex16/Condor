🙏 Thanks for Testing

You're among the first to put Condor through its paces — and we genuinely appreciate you taking it for a spin. Bugs, feedback, and unsolicited opinions are all welcome.

If something breaks, screenshot it. If something delights you, tell us. If a bet calculation feels wrong, you're probably right — and we want to know.

⚠️ Critical: Don't Refresh

This app does not save your data. Everything lives in your current browser session. The only things that will kill your round are refreshing the page or closing the tab — so don't do either until you've settled up.

Take calls, answer texts, snap photos, blast the playlist — your round stays intact. Just come back to this tab when you're ready to enter the next score.

📱 How It Works

1. Select your course — tap the ♥ heart to favorite the tracks you play regularly. They'll float to the top.

2. Set up players — names, handicap indexes, and tee choice per player.

3. Choose your games — Nassau, skins, stroke play, or build fully custom head-to-head matchups with your own stakes.

4. Play — enter scores hole by hole. The board updates live. Hit the Bets tab mid-round to see who's losing their shirt.

5. Settle up — the Settlement screen totals every game and tells you exactly who pays whom. No math, no disputes, no excuses.

6. Brag (or commiserate) — download your scorecard and settlement summary to share with your group chat or immortalize on social media. Victory tastes better with receipts.

⚖️ Terms & Conditions

Condor is a score-tracking and settlement calculator for social golf. It is not a gambling platform, bookmaker, sportsbook, or anything remotely resembling one.

All financial arrangements exist entirely between consenting adults in real life. Condor simply does the arithmetic — the same job as a cocktail napkin, only more reliable and better dressed.

By proceeding you confirm that: (a) you are voluntarily participating in a private, social game among friends; (b) any money exchanged happens entirely outside of and independently from this software; and (c) you won't sue us when your birdie putt lips out on 18.

No data is collected, stored, or transmitted. This is a single-session web app. No accounts. No servers. No drama.
